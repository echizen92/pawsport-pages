01 — Planning

Plans you can actually walk.

Every stop exists. Pawsport checks Google Maps for ratings and opening hours as it plans, so you don't arrive at a museum that shut on Mondays.

  • Each day kept to one or two areas
  • Walking and transit times between stops
  • Drag to reorder, swap anything

02 — Explore

Every city, opened up.

Top sights, food, cafés and markets with photos and ratings, how to get around, and day trips worth taking. Add anything to a day in one tap.

03 — Ask

Ask about anything. It changes the plan.

What's open late near your hotel, whether you need cash, a quieter spot for the afternoon. Pawsport answers, checks the place is open, and adds it when you say so.

04 — Bookings

Forward the email. That's it.

Forward the confirmation, or drop in a PDF or screenshot. Flights, hotels, trains, ferries, cars and tables land on the right day, with the reference to hand.

05 — On the day

Know when to leave.

Where you're going next and how long until you need to leave, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Check in with a tap, or ask Siri what's next.

  • Home Screen widgets for the day
  • Maps and your plan that work with no signal

06 — Afterwards

Come home with the story.

Check in at each stop and add your photos. When you're home there's a logbook and a recap to share, and every place you went is stamped in your passport.

Questions

Good to know.

More in Support.

Is Pawsport on Android?

Not yet. Pawsport is for iPhone. Friends on Android can still join a trip you share: it opens in their browser.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no email and no password. Your trips live on your phone.

Can I trust the visa and entry rules?

Pawsport reads them from official sources and shows where and when it checked. Rules change, so always confirm with the embassy before you fly.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open your profile and tap Delete my data. It happens straight away. See the privacy policy for what's kept and why.
