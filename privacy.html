Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Pawsport plans trips. This page says what it keeps, where, and why — in plain words. The short version: most of your trip lives on your phone; the server keeps only what a feature you use needs; nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.

No account, no tracking

Pawsport doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number to use it. Your phone makes a random identifier the first time it opens, and the server knows you only by that. It is kept in your phone's Keychain, so deleting and reinstalling Pawsport keeps it; to erase what the server holds for it, use Delete my data in the app. The server doesn't store the identifier itself, only a one-way hash of it, so a copy of its records can't be used to act as your phone. People in a group see each other under a stand-in, never each other's identifier. There are no ads, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, stops, notes, bookings, expenses, photos you attach, your packing list, your logbook and your passport details (which country it's from, and its expiry date if you add it) are stored on your device. Your photo library is only read on your phone, to match photos to the places on a trip you planned; photos are not uploaded for this.

Who else sees what

Pawsport uses a few services to do its job, and sends each only what that job needs:

  • AI providers — the text of a request (for example "5 days in Kyoto, mostly food", a booking confirmation you import, or a plan you paste from another assistant) is sent to the AI model that answers it. For entry rules, only the two countries are sent, to a model that searches official sources. When you ask about a trip or a place on it, the trip's plan, the hotels and bookings for its dates, which nights don't have a stay yet, your passport's country with the entry rules Trip ready found (never its expiry date), who is coming if you set it in How you travel (the number of adults and children's ages), the airport or city you usually fly from, your phone's local time and — during the trip — your rounded position go with the question, so the answer can be about where you are, where you are staying, what you need to get in, and booking links can be set for everyone going. When you ask in a group, the names of the people in it, its recent bills and balances, its common fund, open polls and shared plan go with the question, so the answer — or the bill, poll or suggestion it fills in for you to check — is about that group. None of it is sent with your identifier, and answers that use your own bookings are not kept to answer anyone else.
  • Place and map data — place names are looked up with OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and OpenFreeMap), Wikipedia/Wikimedia and Google Places, for search, maps, opening hours and photos.
  • Weather — the forecast, and what a week is usually like, come from Apple Weather. The location of the place on your trip and the date are sent for it — not where you are, and not your identifier. (Earlier versions of the app look this up with Open-Meteo, through Pawsport's server.)
  • Email — forwarded confirmations arrive through our email provider, AgentMail.
  • Trip.com and 12Go — when you tap a link to book, you go through Pawsport's server to Trip.com (stays, flights, flight + hotel, trains, buses, eSIMs, airport transfers, tickets, hire cars, cruises) or, for trains, buses and ferries in Southeast Asia, Hong Kong and Macau, to 12Go. They see your visit as coming from Pawsport, and Pawsport may earn a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you. Pawsport sends them only the search itself: the places, the dates and, if you set who is coming in How you travel, the number of adults and children's ages — nothing about who you are. What you book there is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • Apple — purchases are made and managed by Apple. Pawsport receives a confirmation that you have Pro, never your payment details. A purchase carries a token made one way from your identifier, so Pawsport can tell the phone that bought it; the token doesn't reveal the identifier.

Anything you share on purpose — a trip link, a group invite, a guide, a cat — is visible to the people you share it with, or to everyone if it is public.

Your choices

  • Delete a trip, booking or cat in the app and it is deleted from the server too.
  • Stop sharing your location from the group at any time, and your position is deleted from the server straight away.
  • Remove your profile picture in the app and it is deleted from the server too.
  • Sign out any browser from Plan on the web in the app. Pawsport on the web is at pawsport.kachingz.com/app.
  • If you think your identifier has got out, open your profile in the app and tap Reset this phone's identity. Your phone gets a new identifier and everything moves to it — groups, bills, synced trips, your forwarding address and Pro — and every browser signed in from your phone is signed out.
  • To erase everything the server holds for your phone, open your profile in the app and tap Delete my data. It happens straight away. Groups you share with other people stay for them, without you in them, and expenses you added stay in their split. Your trips on the phone are kept until you delete them there. If you have Pro, tap Restore to bring it back.
