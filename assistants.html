For ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants

Plan with your assistant. Travel with Pawsport.

Add Pawsport to ChatGPT or Claude and ask for your trip as you normally would. Your assistant looks up each place; Pawsport checks every one on the map, says which didn't check out, and hands you a link that opens the trip in Pawsport.

Connector address
https://pawsport.kachingz.com/pawsport/mcp
Sign-in: noneWorks with: ChatGPT · Claude · any MCP client

Add it

  1. Claude (web or desktop) — open Settings (or Customize) → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it Pawsport, paste the address, and choose Add. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds it for the organisation first.
  2. ChatGPT (web) — open Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create, name it Pawsport, paste the address, set authentication to No authentication, and create it. In a Business workspace an admin may need to allow it.
  3. Claude Code — run claude mcp add --transport http pawsport https://pawsport.kachingz.com/pawsport/mcp

The menus move from time to time; if a name doesn't match, look for “connectors” or “apps” in your assistant's settings.

Then ask

Plan 5 days in Shanghai and Hangzhou for two of us — food, old streets, one day in the tea hills. Put it in Pawsport.

Done — 18 of 19 places checked out. Lao Wang's noodles wasn't found, so I swapped in Wei Xiang Zhai. Here's your link: pawsport.kachingz.com/app/?import=…

Open the link on your phone or computer. You'll see the trip day by day on a map, with anything worth a second look marked. On iPhone, copy the link and paste it into Pawsport (new trip → Other ways → From ChatGPT, Gemini or Muse); with Pawsport Pro, Add to my trips on the page puts it on your phone too.

What Pawsport checks

WhereHow each place is found
Most of the worldIts listing on Google Maps, by name and city. A place that has closed for good, or that turns up far from the rest of the trip, is flagged.
Mainland ChinaGoogle lists few shops and restaurants there, so your assistant also gives the Chinese name and the Chinese address down to the door number (号), from Amap or Dianping — and Pawsport places it at that building. An Amap link works too. The Chinese name and address go on the stop, ready to show a taxi driver.
Anything it can't findShown as not found, never dropped or guessed — and your assistant is told what to look up again.

No connector? Use the prompt

Any assistant can write a plan Pawsport reads. Paste this at the end of your request, then paste the answer into Pawsport (new trip → Other ways → From ChatGPT, Gemini or Muse) or into Pawsport on the web.

Plan my trip, then give me the final itinerary as one JSON code block in the Pawsport itinerary format (pawsport-itinerary/1) so I can import it into the Pawsport app.

Format:
{"format":"pawsport-itinerary/1","title":"…","destination":"City, Country","start_date":"YYYY-MM-DD","days":[{"day":1,"date":"YYYY-MM-DD","stops":[{"name":"Name as on Google Maps","local_name":"name in local script","city":"…","address":"street address","local_address":"address in local script","time":"HH:MM","slot":"morning|afternoon|evening","category":"sight|food|cafe|shopping|nightlife|nature|museum|activity|stay|transport|other","map_link":"","notes":"…"}]}]}

Rules: one real place per stop, by its exact Google Maps name. Search the web to check each place exists and is open. Never invent addresses, links or coordinates — leave a field out if unsure. For mainland China, find each place's Chinese name and its full Chinese address down to the door number (号) on Amap or Dianping and put them in local_name and local_address. Only include map_link if I gave you the link or you copied it from a page.

Or a spreadsheet

Prefer a sheet? Download the template and import it from Other ways → From a file you already have. Only Place is needed. For mainland China, fill in Local name and Local address in Chinese, or paste an Amap link in Map link. Sheets made from the older six-column template still import.

Privacy

The connector has no sign-in and doesn't know who you are. Pawsport keeps the itinerary your assistant sends — places, days, times and notes — for 30 days so the link works, and checks its places with Google and Amap. Pawsport doesn't run your request through an AI of its own; your conversation stays with your assistant, under its own privacy policy. Details are on the privacy page.
